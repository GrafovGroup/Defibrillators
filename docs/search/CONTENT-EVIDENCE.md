Keep non-public supplier terms, personal customer information and permissions outside the public repository. Publish only the approved facts/assets. Use honest review dates after checking the source; do not automatically update dates on every build. Cross-link the new evidence from relevant product, service and location pages, then use the normal verified-release IndexNow step.

Monitor Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools for query impressions, crawl/indexing exclusions and sitemap status, and relate those reports to actual Formspree enquiries. A submitted URL, an HTTP 200 IndexNow response or a simulated crawler request is not proof of ranking, an AI citation or a verified search-engine visit.
